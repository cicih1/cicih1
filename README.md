## About Me

I am an undergraduate researcher at **Tongji University**, pursuing a B.Eng. in **Intelligent Manufacturing Engineering**. My research focuses on **medical robotics**, especially autonomous robotic systems that integrate learning-based control, mechanical design, surgical perception, and real-world experimental validation.

My current work centers on building safer and more reliable robotic systems for clinically relevant procedures, including autonomous endovascular guidewire navigation, vision-based interventional robot perception, and intelligent medical device design.

- **Education:** Tongji University, B.Eng. in Intelligent Manufacturing Engineering, expected Jun. 2027
- **GPA:** Major GPA **95/100, 5.0/5.0**; Overall GPA **88.6/100, 4.36/5.0**
- **Research direction:** Medical robotics, autonomous intervention, robot learning, sim-to-real validation, surgical vision
- **Research profile:** 3 years of programming experience, 2 years of mechanical design experience, and 2 years of medical robotics research experience
